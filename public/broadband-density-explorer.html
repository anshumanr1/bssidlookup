About this data

Each tile is a zoom-16 web map tile, about 610 m across at the equator and a little smaller in India. Speed figures are averages of all Speedtest results in the tile during the quarter, across all ISPs. "Fixed" means tests over Wi-Fi or ethernet, so they partly measure home Wi-Fi; "mobile" means 4G or 5G.

Buildings and places come from Overture Maps. Only each feature's bounding box and a few attributes are read, never the full shapes, and they are counted per tile. Building outlines in India are mostly machine-detected from imagery; storey and use tags are sparse.

Everything is read directly from Ookla's and Overture's public files each time you load an area and kept in memory for this session only.

Saved areas and saved weightings are kept in this browser on this computer (local storage). They hold only names, map coordinates, a quarter and weights; no speed, building or place data is saved.
